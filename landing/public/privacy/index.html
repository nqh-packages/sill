Privacy Policy

Sill Tactile (com.uxheavy.sill) is an open-source fork of Noah Saffer’s Sill.

The app connects directly from your iPhone or iPad to your own Mac over your local network, USB cable, or your own VPN. It has no accounts, no ads, no third-party analytics, no telemetry, and no developer servers. Pairing keys and saved Mac records stay exclusively on your device in the iOS Keychain.

For questions or feedback, open an issue on github.com/nqh-packages/sill or email rmdngo@gmail.com.
