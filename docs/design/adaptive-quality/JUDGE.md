## Recommended Base: **Candidate A**

Candidate A is the clearly stronger base (**26/30 vs 20/30**):

1. **Solves the hard product & control-loop problems up front:** Resolves the home-Extreme vs conservative-pace tension with evidence-backed math (one-shot restart cap to `LinkJudge.suggestion`, 1-rung live thereafter), adds loaded-frame climb gating (`fps/4`) and failed-climb exponential hold (10 → 60 s) to prevent still-window false climbs and 8 s restart oscillation.
2. **Deepest, cleanest architecture:** One owner (`AutoQuality`) with a tiny public surface, one running-state commit point (`commitRunning`), deletes `HostConfig.effective(away:)` so there is one way to read what runs, collapses both iOS Quality pickers into `QualityMenu`, and enforces downward-only live mutation in `HEVCEncoder.lowerBitrate(to:)`.
3. **Immediately executable proof:** Both standalone and `-D REPO` `swiftc` checks already pass, and its `Scripts/pacing/` harness scenarios (`autodown`, `autodrift`, `autoclimb`, `autostill`) fit the real no-encoder harness architecture cleanly.
