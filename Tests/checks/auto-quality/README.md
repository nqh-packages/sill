# `auto-quality`

**Checks:** Auto's rung policy (docs/adaptive-quality-plan.md), driven through `close` alone: the ladder (Retina skips Low · Retina, Standard has no Retina, the top is Extreme), the home start at Extreme and the away start at Low · Standard, behind at 3 short seconds of 5 and one rung down live, nothing at 2 of 5, the start's cap by the carried rate (one restart, off with the switch, spent by the first judgement or climb), Efficient · Retina to Low · Standard by restart and nothing below the bottom, a climb after 5 clean seconds in which the stream offered at least fps/4 frames (not 4, not still ones), the doubling hold after a failed climb up to 60 s, no judging while a restart is pending and windows cleared when the running rung changes, which devices govern (home over away, the slowest when all are away, a climb needs every one clean), the host's lines word for word, and a one-line link model whose steps set the pacing harness's gates (24 Mbit/s from Extreme: cap to Balanced, climb to High, Pro does not fit, live back to High, Pro's hold 10, 20, 40, 60 s)

**Compiles:** `Sources/SillHost/AutoQuality.swift` and `Sources/SillHost/LinkJudge.swift` with StreamProtocol's `HostSettings.swift` (`build.sh`)
